Privacy Policy for TripSplit

Effective Date: September 13, 2026

TripSplit is a trip expense management application developed by Dhiren Harivadan Pathak. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the TripSplit application.

1. Information We Collect

TripSplit does not require users to provide an email address, mobile phone number, or other contact information to use the application.

The application may store information entered by the user for managing trips and expenses. This may include trip details, expense information, and the names of people or members added to a trip.

2. How We Use Information

Information entered into TripSplit is used only to provide the application's trip and expense management functionality, including calculating and displaying shared trip expenses.

3. Data Sharing

TripSplit does not sell, rent, or share users' trip information or member names with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

4. Third-Party Services

TripSplit does not currently use Firebase, Google AdMob, or other third-party advertising services.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the application. However, no electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Trip and expense information is retained as necessary for the application's functionality. Users can remove information through the application's available features or by removing the application and its locally stored data, where applicable.

7. Children's Privacy

TripSplit is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal contact information from children.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated effective date.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or TripSplit, you may contact the developer through the contact information provided on the Google Play Store listing.

10. Consent

By using TripSplit, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy and the way information is handled as described above.
